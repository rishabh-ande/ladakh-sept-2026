The question

Seven riding days between leaving Leh and returning. The circuit fills all seven. There is no spare day, so one thing has to go.

Both plans arrive in Leh on the 2nd, acclimatise on the 3rd, cross Khardung La to Thoise, ride Turtuk and Thang, take the Shyok valley to Pangong, cross Rezang La and Tsaga La to Hanle, spend a full day on Umling La at 5,798 m, hold the 11th in Leh, and finish at Lamayuru on the 12th. All of that is fixed and identical.

The only question is what happens to one day: Siachen Base Camp, or the ride home through Tso Moriri. Open both, then say which one you want.

Plan 1 — the Siachen Variant

  • A full day from Sumur to Siachen Base Camp and the memorial, with Panamik’s hot springs on the way.
  • Home from Hanle down the Indus through Chumathang. 1,644 km, longest day 250 km, never sleeps above 4,250 m.
  • Costs you Tso Moriri, Tso Kar and Tanglang La.
  • Two booking changes. Every stay already confirmed stands.

Plan 2 — the Tso Moriri Variant

  • Pangong a day earlier, then home from Hanle through Tso Moriri, Tso Kar, Polokongka La and Tanglang La.
  • 1,646 km, longest day 230 km, four high lakes and seven passes over 4,600 m.
  • Costs you Siachen and the Nubra arm past Sumur.

The short version of the argument

  • For Siachen: access is the whole point. Ordinary visitors are stopped short of Base Camp and never see the glacier. This group is not, and that is attached to a person rather than a place — it will not be available on a future trip. The lakes will be.
  • For Tso Moriri: one day buys four places instead of one, including a lake at 4,522 m that plenty of people rate above Pangong, and two more high passes. It is also the gentler shape, with no day over 230 km.
  • Both plans are complete, both are safe, and both finish on Umling La. Nobody gets a worse trip either way.
